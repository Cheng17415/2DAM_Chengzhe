Halico™ Overheads
In the past year, the company called Halico™ experienced many changes regarding its overheads.
While some months rose sharply, other months fell substantially. In today’s news, we will be reviewing the overheads month by month.

For the first two months, the overheads decreased steadily, reaching one of the lowest expense levels of the company.
One possible reason for this was the profits, which plummeted aggressively.

Between March and April, overheads rose drastically, reaching their peak at the end of April.

From May to July, overheads showed a gradual decline. However, in August they dropped sharply, reaching another low point of the year.

During September and October, overheads increased slightly before stabilizing again.
At the end of the year, the overheads leveled off, remaining relatively constant through November and December.
